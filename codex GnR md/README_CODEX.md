## 1. Project purpose
ONH finite-element growth/remodeling research using legacy FEniCS/DOLFIN. The main biological/mechanical model is a simplified single-component CMT-inspired remodeling model. Theta is normalized remaining load-bearing structural content: theta=1 is healthy content; theta<1 is structural loss/atrophy. Theta is not prescribed geometric shrinkage. Geometric volume is measured independently through J=det(F) and tissue integrals.

## 4. Full-tensor status
Preferred principal-strain method: full symmetric Green-Lagrange tensor E=0.5*(F.T*F-I):
```text
[ Exx Exy Exz ]
[ Exy Eyy Eyz ]
[ Exz Eyz Ezz ]
```
The old method took the larger eigenvalue of the x-z 2x2 block and compared it with Eyy, omitting Exy and Eyz. The updated method projects the complete tensor to DG0, maps cell/component DOFs explicitly, constructs symmetric 3x3 matrices, and uses `numpy.linalg.eigvalsh`. Ascending eigenvalues are E3, E2, E1: largest E1, middle E2, smallest E3. The clean script computes the full eigensystem but retains only E1 for the trigger.

[Saved verification](02_full_tensor/implementation/project/results/full_tensor_verification.md) evaluated 81,531 cells at full loading after five continuation increments (production defaults remain 100). Cell-to-DOF mapping, finiteness, symmetry and eigenvalue ordering passed. All-cell mean relative old/full difference was 7.625769%, maximum 80.100842%, and 23.322417% of cells exceeded 10%. In PT, mean relative difference was 13.523798%, 95th percentile 56.970559%, and 34.187509% exceeded 10%. Maximum absolute difference was 0.07498604489240, in PT. Small medians do not make the omitted shear terms negligible.

`E1_full_tensor.py` retains E1/E2/E3 and old-method diagnostics. `E1_full_tensor_clean.py` is the streamlined research reference; the saved clean smoke outputs and preservation-check source remain alongside it. Verification of principal-strain extraction does not validate CG1 against volumetric locking.

## 9. Current constitutive model status
Main current reference: [05_main_model/reference/E1_full_tensor_clean.py](05_main_model/reference/E1_full_tensor_clean.py), an unchanged copy of the full-tensor clean reference. Its SHA-256 is `3b580fbbbc444d1f0d372579628f278bfeb2cae0692402fdccdacdd6dadec530`, identical to the locking investigation's `reference_E1_full_tensor.py` and the archived `3D_ONH/E1_v3_full tensor/E1_full_tensor_clean.py`.

With F=I+grad(u), C=F.T*F, J=det(F), the current energy is:
```text
psi = theta*mu/2 * (J^(-2/3)*tr(C) - 3)
    + theta*lambda/2 * [ln(J)]^2
mu = E/[2*(1+nu)]
lambda = E*nu/[(1+nu)*(1-2*nu)]
```
The code fixes `theta_vol=1` and `beta_volume=0`. Theta scales both distortional and volumetric stiffness, with preferred volume J=1 at every theta. Structural loss does not directly command contraction; any geometric volume change follows equilibrium under loading. For this exact split, the small-strain hydrostatic tangent is lambda, not conventional K=lambda+2*mu/3; retain this distinction from the saved benchmark, without changing it.

Current reference uses CG1 displacement, DG0 theta and tensor postprocessing, six tissues at nu=0.49, Newton/MUMPS, and the existing 100/500/100 loading/remodeling/unloading defaults. Its research-reference designation does not resolve locking or authorize execution.
